Privacy Policy

Gutenberg Elite Home & Property Managements ("we", "us", "our") operates the Rental Messaging & Property Management System at rental.quantumcode.co.ke (the "Service"). This policy explains what we collect, why we collect it, and the choices you have.

1. Information We Collect

  • Tenant details: name, tenant code, phone number, house/unit and property information.
  • Financial records: rent amounts, arrears, deposits, penalties and invoice details, and payment history.
  • Payment references: the M-Pesa reference code attached to each payment transaction.
  • Message records: automated rent reminders and payment confirmations sent to your phone, including delivery status.
  • Usage data: administrator account credentials and activity within the Service.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To collect and record rental payments and allocate them correctly to your account.
  • To send you rent reminders, payment confirmations, and property-related notices.
  • To prepare statements, receipts, invoices, and management reports.
  • To maintain accurate tenancy and accounting records.

3. Sharing of Information

We do not sell your personal information. We share data only with service providers necessary to operate the Service, including:

  • Meta Platforms, Inc. — for delivering WhatsApp messages (templates) to your phone number.
  • Our cloud database and hosting provider (Supabase) — for secure data storage.

4. Data Retention and Security

We retain your records for as long as needed for accounting, tenancy, and legal purposes. Data is stored in encrypted cloud databases with access restricted to authorized administrators. Financial and identity data is never sold or shared for advertising.

5. Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data held in the Service, subject to applicable law and the need to retain accounting records. To exercise these rights, contact the administrator through your landlord or property manager.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Effective date" at the top indicates the latest revision. Continued use of the Service after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

7. Contact

For privacy-related questions, contact us through your property manager or landlord, or via the Service website at rental.quantumcode.co.ke.
